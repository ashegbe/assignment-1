Welcome to GSOCollective!

Our purpose:
Our purpose at GSOCollective, is to connect individuals and families in Greensboro through events, community service, and opportunity. We hold ocassions every Friday and Saturday in Downtown Greensboro as a way to unwind, mingle, and make a difference in your community. So, pop out and get a taste of the Greensboro Culture!
