Community Spotlight: Art Workshop

Date and Time

Friday, September 4, 2026, 6-8 pm

Event Overview:

Come this Friday to hone your artistry! This art workshop is an open space for all artists to come and do what they love. If you love recieving feedback, we have coaches on site to give critique on your work and help you improve creatively. If you don't have materials, don't worry, our creative studio has all the materials you will need. All we ask from you, is to come, share, and create!

Featured Activities

  1. Literature - Fiction, Short Story, Poetry
  2. Sketching- Figure drawing, cartooning, portraits
  3. Painting- Surrealism, Landscape, Still life
  4. Design- Marketing, Motion Graphics, and Editing
  5. Musical Expression - Piano, Guitar, Producing

Attendee tips

  • Parking is free in the parking lot of the studio.
  • Arrive 15-30 minutes early to allow time to check in.
  • Check the website for parking information and available parking lots.
  • Register your name at the front door.
